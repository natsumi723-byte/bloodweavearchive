阳光不再眷顾他

■ 源文件简介

This is where creatures of the night: illithid player, emperor, and Astarion states are dealt with. Could likely start on the docks, would be nice to start them walking back to the warehouse area at Trunk Point to get a staging change into the scene. EXITS: END_BrainBattle_CombatOver_Nested_Conclusion Terminus

参考译文这一段处理属于夜行者的几种状态，包括夺心魔玩家、君主与阿斯代伦。场景可以从码头开始，让众人走回仓库区域，在主干点转换走位。出口为终战后的结尾段或终止节点。
