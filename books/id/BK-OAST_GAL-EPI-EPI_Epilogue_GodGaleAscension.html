野心神归来

■ 源文件简介

Gale successfully became the god of ambition, and has spent the last six months establishing his domain, seeking new followers, and otherwise acclimatising to godhood. No one has heard from him during that time. His posture/demeanor here should feel slightly more aloof/detached than the regular Gale - he's been immortal for six months, his ego is as powerful as his magic. The real Gale's insecurities still lurk beneath his godlike confidence, as does his love for the player, but this is clearly a Gale setting out on a darker path. He will physically look like a god, and hopefully have some voice processing to add further godliness. Withers has just delivered the final toast, and the party will soon disband. For players who said they wanted to go with Gale and become a god - this is the moment they can choose to go with him.

参考译文盖尔成功成为野心神，并在过去六个月里建立自己的神域、寻找新的追随者，同时适应神明身份。这段时间里，没有人收到过他的消息。他在这里的姿态与举止应当比凡人盖尔略显疏离——他已经拥有六个月的不朽生命，自负也变得与魔力同样强大。原本属于盖尔的不安全感仍藏在神明般的自信之下，他对起源阿斯代伦的爱也依然存在；但这个盖尔显然正走上一条更黑暗的道路。他在外表上会呈现神明形态，并希望通过声音处理进一步增强神性。此时，守墓人刚刚致完最后一轮祝酒辞，聚会即将散场；曾表示想与盖尔同行并成为神明的起源阿斯代伦，可以在这一刻选择随他离开。
