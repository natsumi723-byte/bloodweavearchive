与你共享的未来

■ 源文件简介

This dialogue covers instances where Gale attends the Withers party in a human (non-god) form. His default state is that he returned to Waterdeep and became a professor of Illusory magic at his former school, Blackstaff Academy. General vibe here is that this a Gale who's found peace with himself - he's a great teacher, one his students are mostly in awe of. If he's not a teacher in Waterdeep, he could be in several other states (living in Baldur's Gate, living in the hells, living in the underdark etc) but the general vibe is that he's always happy with where he's ended up, as in those cases he's there because that's where his romance partner chose to be.

参考译文这段对话涵盖盖尔以凡人而非神明形态参加守墓人聚会的情况。默认状态下，他回到深水城，在自己的母校黑杖学院担任幻术教授。这里整体呈现的是已经与自己和解的盖尔：他是一位出色的老师，学生大多对他心怀敬畏。若他没有在深水城教书，也可能处于其他几种状态，例如生活在博德之门、地狱或幽暗地域；但总体而言，他始终满意自己最终抵达的地方，因为在这些情况下，他会在那里，是由于恋爱伴侣选择了那里。
